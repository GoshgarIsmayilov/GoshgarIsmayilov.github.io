Hi! I am an independent scholar, having received my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Boğaziçi University, advised by Prof. Can Ozturan. I currently serve as Vice Team Lead at BNP Paribas – Turkish Economy Bank (TEB), with 7+ years of industry experience spanning fintech, on-demand logistics, and defense technology. Previously, I received my M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Marmara University, advised by Prof. Haluk Rahmi Topcuoglu.

I am open to academic collaboration – please feel free to reach out via email: goshgar.ismayilov@boun.edu.tr.

Research

My research interests span the following three directions:

  • Privacy-preserving protocols on blockchain with zero-knowledge proofs (JPDC '26, Comput. Commun. '25, JNCA '25)
  • LLM-based agentic workflows on blockchain (ICAIT '26, BdKCSE '25)
  • Dynamic multi-objective optimization with evolutionary computation and neural networks (FGCS '20, DSP '23)
